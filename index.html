How to use

  1. Choose a viewpoint. Search for a location, use a geotagged photo, or your current position.
  2. Explore the terrain. Switch between the satellite map, 3D terrain, a ground-level viewpoint, and a walk through the landscape.
  3. Read the skyline. Drag to look around. Tap a ridge for its coordinates, distance, and altitude.
Map & movement controls

Map: drag to pan. Terrain: drag to orbit, Shift-drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. Walk: WASD or arrows to move, E/Q to ascend or descend. Escape returns to the map.
