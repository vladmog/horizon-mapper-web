Horizon Mapper privacy

Horizon Mapper has no application backend, user accounts, analytics, advertising, or remote error logging. Photos and GPX files are read locally in your browser and are not uploaded by the app. Projects are saved in your browser and are exported only when you choose to download them.

Mapping a location requires direct requests to third-party public data services. AWS Open Data receives terrain-tile coordinates, Esri receives imagery-tile coordinates, and the OpenStreetMap Overpass service receives a nearby-peak search area. Those providers can infer the approximate area being viewed from those requests and apply their own privacy policies.

Device location and motion access occur only after browser permission. Cached terrain and project data can be removed by clearing this site's data in the browser.
